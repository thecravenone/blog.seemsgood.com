# Ingredients

* 3/4 cup butter
* 3/4 cup flour
* 2 quarts high-quality stock ([below](#stock)), warm
* 1 Tablespoon Worcestershire Sauce
* 1/2 Tablespoon Soy Sauce
* 1/2 Tablespoon Fish Sauce (Substitution: more Soy Sauce)
* 4 Sprigs Rosemary
* 4 Sprigs Thyme
* 4 Leaves Sage
* 1/2 Tablespoon Freshly Ground Black Pepper
* 1 Teaspoon Kosher Salt

# Method

In a large saucepan, melt butter over medium heat. Add flour and whisk until combined. Continue whisking until raw flour smell disapates. Roux should be roughly the color of [Café au Lait](https://www.pantone.com/hk/en-hk/color-finder/17-1227-tcx).

Add stock one ladleful at a time, whisking until combined.

Add remaining ingredients and stir.

Bring to a low simmer. The longer it simmers, the thicker and stronger tasting it will become.

Strain into a warmed serving vessel.

# Stock

## Ingredients

* 1 Roasted Poultry Carcass[^1]
* 1 Tablespoon Neutral Oil (such as Canola)
* 1 Onion
* 4 Ribs Celery
* 2 Large carrots
* 1 Tablespoon Tomato Paste
* 2 Quarts Water



## Method

Thinly slice all vegetables.

In a pressure cooker, add oil and vegetables and sauté over high heat until onions are transluscent on their edges. Add tomato paste and sauté until tomato smell dissapates and tomato paste darkens.

Add water and carcass to pressure cooker.

Cook on high pressure for 45 minutes. Allow pressure to natually release.

Strain solids from stock and skim any fat.

[^1]: For today, I've used a Costco rotisserie chicken. For Thanksgiving, I'll use a full turkey carcass.
