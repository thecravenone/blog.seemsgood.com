**Milburn Memorial**

>If not for the contributions made by. the women of Seattle, there would be no Washington Park Arboretum.
>
>Anna Thomsen Milburn, to whom this memorial was dedicated, was a pioneer for civic leadership. Among her many accomplishments, Milburn served on the Seattle Parks Board, and presided over the Seattle Garden Club and the Arboretum Foundation.
In 1940, she became one of the first women to run for U.S. President when she was nominated by the National Greenback Party.

This faces what is probably a pretty good vista most of the time but unfortunately was under construction.
