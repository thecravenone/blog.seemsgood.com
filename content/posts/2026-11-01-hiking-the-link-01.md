**Hamlin Park**

Hamlin Park was a real treat. There's a bazillion crisscrossing trails and one could easily just wander around without worrying about being too far from getting out when ready. I'm learning now that those markers I was seeing all over the place are part of [a permanent orienteering course](https://cascadeoc.org/map/hamlin-park/) that runs throughout the park.
