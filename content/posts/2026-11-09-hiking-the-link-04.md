Today's urban hike was 4.3 miles from Mountlake Terrace to Lynnwood City Center via Lake Ballinger and The Interurban Trail.

[![](/images/2026-11-09-map.jpg)](/images/2026-11-09-map.jpg)

On a clear day, the views from the platform are pretty great as you can see the Cascades to the east and the Olympics to the west. Unfortunately, this was not a clear day.
